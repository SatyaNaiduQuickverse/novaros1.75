Sequence
1Accelerometer offset & scale — removes the ~10° error in “down”
2Axis map vs the flight controller — which body axis is which, and the signs
3Independent check — level reads 0°/0°, tilts track, correction opposes

Both calibrations are needed and they fix different things. Step 1 first: step 2 picks each axis from the largest raw component, and the offset step 1 removes is easily large enough to mis-call a shallow tilt.
